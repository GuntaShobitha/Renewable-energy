Standard grid-tied solar systems automatically disconnect during an outage for safety reasons (anti-islanding mandate to protect electrical utility linemen). We provide a legally binding linear generation guarantee: your system will produce at least 90% of its rated nameplate capacity at year 10 and at least 87.4% at year 25. If production falls short due to hardware degradation or system faults, we rectify the issue and directly reimburse you for the shortfall. This commitment is backstopped by independent asset reinsurance from Munich Re.
