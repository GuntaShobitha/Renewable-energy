Solar deep dive

How we size your system

Most systems are oversized because it is easier to sell. We start by analysing twelve months of your utility bills, then model your roof's shading, tilt and orientation hour by hour.

Only then do we choose panel count and inverter type, comparing at least two layouts so you can see the trade-off between cost and production.

  • Hour-by-hour production model
  • Shade and structural review
  • Two layout options in every quote

How a project runs

From the first site assessment to system activation, every project follows a structured process designed to keep the work predictable, transparent, and technically sound.

Week 1

Assess & Design

We evaluate your roof, electrical infrastructure, energy usage, and property conditions to engineer a system sized for your actual needs.

Week 2

Permits & Approvals

Our team prepares and submits the required plans, utility interconnection documents, and local permits so the project can move forward without unnecessary delays.

Week 3–4

Install & Commission

Our in-house installation team mounts the system, completes the electrical work, configures monitoring, and commissions the equipment for safe and reliable operation.

Week 5

Inspect & Activate

After final inspection and utility approval, we complete system activation and verify that your solar and energy equipment is operating as designed.

Wind & storage

When wind and batteries make sense

Wind works best on open sites with steady average speeds above about 5 m/s. We place an anemometer for a few weeks before recommending a turbine.

Batteries pay off most where evening rates are high or outages are frequent. We show the numbers both ways so you can decide with real figures.
